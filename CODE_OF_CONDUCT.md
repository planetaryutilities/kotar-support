# Code of Conduct

The Starforge Kotar support repository is intended to provide a professional environment for product support, documentation, bug reporting, and technical discussion.

## Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate professionally and respectfully
- Keep discussions relevant to Starforge Kotar
- Provide constructive technical feedback
- Avoid knowingly misleading or inaccurate reports
- Respect the privacy and intellectual property of others
- Avoid posting sensitive or confidential information
- Follow applicable laws and regulations

---

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Threats or abusive language
- Spam or advertising
- Deliberate disruption of technical discussions
- Publication of another person's private information
- Publication of credentials or security-sensitive information
- Uploading confidential or proprietary customer information
- Uploading export-controlled or classified information
- Repeated submission of knowingly false or misleading reports
- Use of this repository for unrelated activities

---

## Moderation

Repository maintainers may:

- Edit or remove inappropriate content
- Close or lock discussions or issues
- Remove confidential or sensitive information where possible
- Restrict participation by users who repeatedly violate this policy
- Report activity to GitHub where appropriate

Moderation decisions may be made at the maintainers' discretion to protect users, customers, the repository, and the Starforge project.

---

## Security Issues

Potential security vulnerabilities should not be discussed publicly.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the appropriate reporting process.
